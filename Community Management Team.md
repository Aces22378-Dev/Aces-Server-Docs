# COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT TEAM HANDBOOK

**Author:** Its-Aurelia-Dev
**Server Staff Document — COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT TEAM**

## 1. PURPOSE

Community Staff are responsible for helping build a welcoming, active, and healthy community.

Unlike Moderators, whose primary responsibility is rule enforcement, Community Staff focus on **member experience, engagement, communication, events, and community feedback**.

---

# 2. COMMUNITY STAFF RESPONSIBILITIES

Community Staff may be responsible for:

* Organizing community events.
* Gathering member feedback.
* Promoting positive community interaction.
* Assisting with announcements.
* Supporting community projects.
* Identifying recurring community concerns.
* Reporting rule violations to Moderators.
* Communicating community feedback to Admins and Executives.

---

# 3. COMMUNITY CONDUCT

Community Staff must:

* Treat members respectfully.
* Remain approachable.
* Avoid unnecessary arguments.
* Avoid favoritism.
* Keep private member information confidential.
* Follow the same public rules as everyone else.
* Respect Moderator and Administrator decisions.

Community Staff are representatives of the server.

---

# 4. MEMBER SUPPORT

When helping a member:

1. Listen to the problem.
2. Determine whether you can solve it.
3. Provide accurate information.
4. Direct them to the correct staff team when necessary.
5. Avoid promising something you cannot guarantee.

If the issue involves punishment, moderation, or an appeal, refer the member to the appropriate Moderator or Admin.

---

# 5. COMMUNITY FEEDBACK

Community Staff should collect useful feedback rather than simply collecting complaints.

Useful feedback includes:

* What members like.
* What members dislike.
* Common requests.
* Recurring problems.
* Suggestions for improvement.
* Concerns about new features.
* Event feedback.

Feedback should be presented objectively.

---

# 6. EVENTS

Community Staff may organize:

* Community events.
* Competitions.
* Giveaways when authorized.
* Community activities.
* Polls.
* Q&A sessions.
* Special server events.

Events must follow server rules and any applicable platform rules.

Do not organize events involving unsafe, illegal, or inappropriate activities.

---

# 7. ANNOUNCEMENTS

Important announcements should be:

* Accurate.
* Clear.
* Relevant.
* Approved when necessary.
* Posted in the appropriate channel.

Do not announce unreleased information without authorization.

---

# 8. COMMUNITY DRAMA

Community Staff should not fuel drama.

If members are arguing:

* Remain neutral.
* Encourage appropriate communication.
* Notify Moderators if rules are being broken.
* Avoid publicly taking sides.
* Do not spread rumors.

Community Staff should help reduce unnecessary conflict rather than become part of it.

---

# 9. REPORTING RULE VIOLATIONS

Community Staff are not expected to personally handle serious moderation issues unless they have the appropriate permissions.

Instead:

1. Preserve relevant information.
2. Report the issue to a Moderator or Admin.
3. Avoid publicly confronting the person.
4. Do not attempt to punish the member without authorization.

---

# 10. WORKING WITH MODERATORS

Community Staff should work closely with Moderators.

Examples:

**Community Staff:** "Several members are reporting repeated spam in this channel."

**Moderator:** Investigates and handles the rule violation.

Community Staff should provide information rather than attempt to replace the moderation team.

---

# 11. WORKING WITH DEVELOPERS

Community Staff may identify problems with:

* Bots.
* Website features.
* Commands.
* Community tools.
* Event systems.

Technical issues should be reported to Developers through the appropriate system.

---

# 12. WORKING WITH ADMINS

Escalate to Admins when:

* A community issue becomes serious.
* Multiple members are affected.
* A staff conflict develops.
* A major event needs approval.
* A recurring problem requires an administrative decision.
* There is a serious complaint about another staff member.

---

# 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

Community Staff may receive private information from members.

Do not:

* Share private conversations unnecessarily.
* Post screenshots publicly.
* Spread rumors.
* Reveal private reports.
* Share personal information.

If information needs to be escalated, send it only to the appropriate staff members.

---

# 14. COMMUNITY PROJECTS

Community Staff may propose:

* New events.
* New channels.
* Community programs.
* Feedback systems.
* Member activities.
* Improvement ideas.

Major structural changes should be approved by the appropriate Admin or Executive.

---

# 15. COMMUNITY STAFF CONDUCT

Community Staff must not:

* Abuse their position.
* Favor friends.
* Harass members.
* Leak confidential information.
* Pretend to have permissions they do not have.
* Make unauthorized promises.
* Start or encourage community drama.
* Use the position for personal gain.

---

# 16. FINAL STANDARD

Community Staff help shape the culture of the server.

A good Community Staff member is approachable, reliable, respectful, organized, and focused on making the community better.
